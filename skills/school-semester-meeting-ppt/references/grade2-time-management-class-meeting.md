## 1. Route and Learning Outcome

Use this reference for Grade 2 student-facing class meetings about time awareness, task order, pre-class preparation, recess planning, homework planning, or regular sleep and wake habits.

Default title:

> 《成为时间管理小达人》
> 会看时间，更会安排时间

Default use:

- Audience: Grade 2 students.
- Use case: opening class meeting or study-habit lesson.
- Length: 20 slides and 30-35 minutes.
- Culminating artifact: a personal homework schedule.

Learning goals:

1. Understand that time keeps moving and needs to be arranged.
2. Experience one minute and estimate simple task durations.
3. Use the principle "先重要，后轻松；一件一件来。"
4. Practise pre-class preparation and sensible recess planning.
5. Estimate homework duration and order homework tasks.
6. Prepare for the next day and maintain regular sleep and wake habits.
7. Complete a simple homework schedule.

Do not frame the lesson as productivity pressure. The child should learn to feel calmer, know what comes next, and leave room for rest. Do not praise speed alone or compare completion speed between students.

## 2. NotebookLM Prompt Assembly

When producing a copy-ready NotebookLM prompt, use this exact section order:

1. Basic information.
2. Learning goals.
3. Overall visual style.
4. Page-by-page content.
5. Generation checks.

The prompt must state:

- Generate exactly 20 slides; do not add or remove slides.
- Use 16:9 landscape format.
- Keep all visible language appropriate for Grade 2.
- Include short usable text and a matching illustration on every content slide.
- Keep text and illustrations in separate readable areas.
- Keep one main teaching idea per slide.
- Use two recurring Grade 2 student characters with consistent faces, clothing, and illustration style.
- Do not use corporate-report layouts, dense paragraphs, tiny text, complex charts, training camps, levels, ranking, trophies, or speed competition.
- Do not print prompt labels such as "页面标题", "页面文字", "画面", "互动设计", or "教师备注" on the slides.
- Render interaction cues naturally as small boxes such as "一起想一想" or "一起试一试".
- If speaker notes are supported, add 1-2 teacher guidance sentences per slide and keep them out of the visible slide body.

## 3. Visual System

Use a warm campus-journal picture-book style:

- Palette: sky blue, mint green, sunshine yellow, warm white.
- Background: light solid or near-solid colors.
- Supporting motifs: clocks, calendars, schoolbags, pencils, exercise books, the sun, the moon, and simple timelines.
- Scene types: home morning routine, school arrival, classroom, corridor, recess, family study corner, and bedtime.
- Illustration: clean child-friendly picture-book or flat illustration, lively but not preschool-like.
- Typography: large readable Chinese type suitable for classroom projection.
- Composition: one dominant scene plus a short text block; avoid body text over busy images.

## 4. Canonical 20-Slide Structure

### Slide 1 — Cover

Visible title:

> 成为时间管理小达人

Visible subtitle:

> 会看时间，更会安排时间

Visual: Two Grade 2 students walk toward school in the morning. Blend a clock, the sun, and a morning-to-night timeline into the background.

### Slide 2 — 同样的一天，为什么不一样？

Visible text:

> 小宇：找不到文具，课前手忙脚乱，作业写到很晚。
>
> 小晴：提前准备，按顺序完成，晚上安心入睡。
>
> 他们拥有的时间一样多，为什么一天过得不一样？

Visual: A left-right comparison of a disorganized day and a calm, ordered day.

Interaction: Ask students to identify where 小宇 lost time.

### Slide 3 — 时间是我们的好朋友

Visible text:

> 时间一直向前走。
>
> 每个人每天拥有的时间一样多。
>
> 我们不能留住时间，但可以安排时间。

Visual: A horizontal timeline from sunrise through school and home to moonlight.

### Slide 4 — 一分钟能做什么？

Visible text:

> 一分钟可以整理几本文具？
>
> 一分钟可以读多少个词语？
>
> 一分钟可以完成几次跳绳？
>
> 认真使用一分钟，也能完成一件小事。

Visual: Three small timed scenes for organizing stationery, reading, and skipping rope, with a large "1分钟".

Interaction: Run a one-minute stationery-organizing or quiet-reading experience.

### Slide 5 — 估一估，需要多长时间？

Visible text:

> 刷牙需要多久？
>
> 整理书包需要多久？
>
> 完成一道口算题需要多久？
>
> 读完一小段文字需要多久？
>
> 先估一估，再实际看一看。

Visual: Four task cards with small clock icons.

Interaction: Students choose one task, estimate the time, and explain why.

### Slide 6 — 安排时间，先分清先后

Visible text:

> 先做必须完成的事。
>
> 再做自己想做的事。
>
> 做完一件，再开始下一件。
>
> 先重要，后轻松；一件一件来。

Visual: Three task cards connected by clear arrows.

### Slide 7 — 早晨不慌张的小秘密

Visible text:

> 1. 按时起床
>
> 2. 洗漱穿戴
>
> 3. 吃好早餐
>
> 4. 检查书包
>
> 5. 准时出发

Visual: A five-panel sequence from getting up to leaving with a schoolbag.

Interaction: Let students arrange the five steps in order.

### Slide 8 — 到校以后，我先做什么？

Visible text:

> 放好书包和水杯。
>
> 交好需要上交的作业。
>
> 拿出第一节课的学习用品。
>
> 安静等待上课。

Visual: A bright classroom with orderly placement of schoolbag, water bottle, homework, and books.

### Slide 9 — 课前三分钟准备法

Visible text:

> 第一步：看课程表。
>
> 第二步：拿出课本、练习本和文具。
>
> 第三步：整理桌面，安静等候。
>
> 看一看，拿一拿，摆一摆。

Visual: A large number 3, a countdown clock, and three consecutive preparation actions.

Interaction: Rehearse the three-minute preparation routine as a class.

### Slide 13 — 写作业前，先做好准备

### Slide 14 — 作业四步法

Visible text:

> 一看：看清任务。
>
> 二排：排好顺序。
>
> 三做：一次完成一项。
>
> 四查：检查并收好。
>
> 看清楚，排好队，认真做，再检查。

Visual: Four continuous task cards or footsteps without level-clearing imagery.

Interaction: Show reading, math practice, and schoolbag organization; ask which one students would do first and why.

### Slide 15 — 给作业做一个小计划

Visible text:

> 今天有哪些任务？
>
> 每项任务大约需要多久？
>
> 我准备先做什么？
>
> 完成一项，打一个勾。

Example:

> 语文朗读——预计10分钟
>
> 数学练习——预计15分钟
>
> 整理书包——预计5分钟

Visual: A simple task, estimated-time, and completion checklist.

### Slide 16 — 专心一小段，效率更高

Visible text:

> 开始前，收起无关的玩具。
>
> 学习时，只做眼前这一项。
>
> 完成一项，再短暂休息。
>
> 遇到困难，先思考，再求助。

Visual: A student focused at a tidy desk with toys and electronic devices put away.

Interaction: Ask students what most often distracts them and name one solution.

### Slide 17 — 睡前十分钟，准备明天

Visible text:

> 按照课程表整理书包。
>
> 准备第二天要穿的衣物。
>
> 放好需要携带的物品。
>
> 完成后，再检查一次。
>
> 今晚准备好，明早不慌张。

Visual: A warm evening scene with timetable, schoolbag, clothes, and water bottle placed in order.

Interaction: Use a "找遗漏" scene so students identify the missing item.

### Slide 18 — 早睡早起，身体有精神

Visible text:

> 每天尽量按时睡觉、按时起床。
>
> 睡前不拖拉，不把作业留到最后。
>
> 保证充足睡眠，第二天更有精神。
>
> 早晨留出时间，不慌不忙地出门。

Visual: Two connected scenes showing peaceful sleep and energetic waking.

### Slide 19 — 制作我的写作业时间表

Visible text:

> 第一步：写下今天的作业。
>
> 第二步：估算每项作业需要的时间。
>
> 第三步：安排开始时间和完成顺序。
>
> 第四步：完成一项，打一个勾。
>
> 第五步：比较预计用时和实际用时。

Include the exact table defined in **Homework Schedule Artifact** below.

Visible reminder:

> 时间表不是让我们一直写作业，而是帮助我们知道先做什么、什么时候可以安心休息。

Visual: A home study desk with the schedule enlarged as the main readable object. Use clock, exercise-book, pencil, and checkbox motifs.

Interaction: Students fill in a schedule from sample homework, then check task order, checking time, and rest time with a partner.

### Slide 20 — 我的时间管理约定

Visible text:

> 我会看时间。
>
> 我会分清先后。
>

>

>

>
> 管理好时间，做时间的小主人。

Interaction: Each student chooses one time habit to practise this week and signs a commitment card.

## 5. Homework Schedule Artifact

Slide 19 must use the title **制作我的写作业时间表**. Do not replace it with "制作我的一日时间表".

Required columns:

| 作业任务 | 开始时间 | 预计用时 | 实际用时 | 完成 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 语文朗读 | 18:30 | 10分钟 | ____分钟 | □ |
| 数学练习 | 18:40 | 15分钟 | ____分钟 | □ |
| 休息活动 | 18:55 | 5分钟 | ____分钟 | □ |
| 检查订正 | 19:00 | 10分钟 | ____分钟 | □ |
| 整理书包 | 19:10 | 5分钟 | ____分钟 | □ |

Treat these times as editable examples. If the user's school or family schedule differs, preserve the columns and planning logic while adjusting the sample times.

## 6. Final Checks

Before returning a framework, prompt, or deck, verify:

- The deck contains exactly 20 slides unless the user explicitly requested another count.
- Slide 19 is the homework schedule, not a whole-day schedule.
- The narrative covers time awareness, duration, priority, pre-class preparation, recess, homework planning, next-day preparation, and sleep rhythm.
- Every content slide has readable text and a matching school or home illustration.
- All language is concrete and understandable for Grade 2.
- The two recurring student characters remain consistent.
- Interactions appear naturally every 2-3 pages without turning the lesson into a competition.
- The lesson values calm planning, completion, checking, and rest rather than speed.
- Structural prompt labels do not appear in rendered slides.
- Speaker notes, when supported, stay outside the visible slide body.
