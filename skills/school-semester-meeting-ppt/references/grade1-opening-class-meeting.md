## 1. Market-informed decisions

Use the following as durable product guidance, not as a claim that engagement equals teaching quality:

- The strongest demand clusters around "我是小学生啦", kindergarten-to-primary transition, school routines, campus safety, and a complete first lesson.
- Teachers respond to concrete coverage: teachers and classmates, campus places, bells, sitting, listening, raising a hand, queueing, recess, schoolbag organization, safety, friendship, and help-seeking.
- "可编辑", "直接用", "含教案", and "含逐字稿" reduce preparation risk and are stronger purchase signals than generic educational slogans.
- Comment sections can be dominated by "求课件", "求分享", or delivery requests. Record this as resource demand; do not describe it as independent proof of pedagogical quality.
- Emotional reassurance differentiates the product. Begin with curiosity, nervousness, belonging, or a relatable first-day story before presenting rules.
- Light activities improve usability. Prefer feeling choices, self-introduction, posture imitation, scene judgement, queue practice, and polite-language rehearsal.
- Avoid overusing training-camp, mission, badge, checkpoint, or level-clearing language. The course should feel like a warm campus journey.

## 2. Default product direction

Use this default when the user asks for a Grade 1 opening class meeting without a fixed concept:

- **Title:** 《你好，一年级！开启校园成长之旅》
- **Audience:** Grade 1 pupils, approximately ages 6-7.
- **Use case:** Opening class meeting, first lesson, or kindergarten-to-primary transition.
- **Length:** 30 slides, 16:9.
- **Narrative:** Follow a child's first school day rather than a sequence of unrelated rules.
- **Learning route:** feelings → identity → campus → people → classroom routines → recess → safety → friendship → help-seeking → reading/tasks → promise.
- **Tone:** warm, reassuring, concrete, and teacher-ready.
- **Interaction rhythm:** one light interaction every 2-3 slides.

When the user wants a different title, preserve the route unless the new concept requires a different structure.

## 3. Visual system

- Use a bright, warm campus picture-book illustration style.
- Use warm white, sky blue, tender green, pale yellow, and soft orange.
- Keep one consistent boy and one consistent girl as recurring Grade 1 characters.
- Use real school scenes: gate, classroom, corridor, stairs, playground, reading corner, desk, and home study area.
- Separate text and illustration. Do not place body text over a busy illustration.
- Use Microsoft YaHei or another projection-safe Chinese font.
- Use a large rounded display font only for short titles.
- Keep each slide to one idea and no more than four short visible text points.
- Avoid corporate dashboards, heavy gradients, complex textures, fantasy adventure maps, and decoration overload.
- Use a small "试一试" marker for interactions; do not present it as a level or checkpoint.

## 4. Canonical 30-page framework

Use the visible wording below as a reliable starting point. Adapt class names, school routines, and safety details when the user provides local requirements.

### 1. Cover

- Title: 你好，一年级！
- Subtitle: 开启校园成长之旅
- Small text: 一年级开学第一课
- Visual: pupils carrying schoolbags toward a sunny primary school; teacher welcomes them at the gate.

### 2. Welcome

- Title: 欢迎你，小学生！
- Text: 新教室 / 新老师 / 新伙伴 / 新的开始
- Visual: pupils entering a bright classroom.
- Interaction: wave to the teacher and a nearby classmate.

### 3. Feeling check-in

- Title: 今天，你是什么心情？
- Text: 开心 / 期待 / 紧张 / 有一点担心
- Closing: 每一种心情，都可以被看见。
- Visual: sunny, rainbow, cloudy, and light-rain feeling icons.
- Interaction: choose a "feeling weather".

### 4. New identity

- Title: 从今天起，我是一名小学生
- Text: 我有自己的班级 / 我有新的老师和同学 / 我会学习新的本领 / 我会照顾好自己
- Visual: child seeing a confident primary-school self in a mirror.

### 5. What changes

- Title: 小学生活有什么不同？
- Text: 有清脆的上课铃 / 有整齐的课桌椅 / 有新的课本和任务 / 有丰富的校园生活
- Visual: gentle kindergarten and primary-school comparison.
- Interaction: find new elements of primary-school life.

### 6. Campus places

- Title: 认识我们的新校园
- Text: 校门 / 教学楼 / 教室 / 操场 / 卫生间 / 阅读角
- Visual: simple campus overview with clear icons, not an adventure map.

### 7. Meet the teacher

- Title: 老师是学校里帮助我的人
- Text: 不会的问题，可以问老师 / 身体不舒服，要告诉老师 / 心里难过，也可以找老师
- Visual: teacher listening at the child's eye level.

### 8. Meet classmates

- Title: 你好，新同学！
- Text: 你好，我叫____ / 我喜欢____ / 很高兴认识你
- Visual: two pupils introducing themselves.
- Interaction: introduce yourself to a partner.

### 9. The school-day rhythm

- Title: 小学生的一天
- Text: 到校问好 / 认真上课 / 快乐课间 / 整齐排队 / 安全放学
- Visual: horizontal five-scene timeline.

### 10. Understand the bell

- Title: 听懂校园里的铃声
- Text: 预备铃—准备课本和学具 / 上课铃—坐好，安静等待 / 下课铃—收好物品，有序休息
- Visual: pupils responding correctly to different bells.
- Interaction: imitate the correct action after a bell cue.

### 11. Sitting posture

- Title: 坐端正，精神好
- Text: 头正 / 肩平 / 背直 / 脚放稳
- Visual: clear correct posture with a small incorrect comparison.
- Interaction: practise the posture together.

### 12. Listening

- Title: 认真听，我能学得更好
- Text: 小眼睛，看老师 / 小耳朵，认真听 / 小脑袋，跟着想 / 不随便打断别人
- Visual: attentive classroom with eye, ear, and thinking icons.

### 13. Raising a hand

- Title: 先举手，再发言
- Text: 我有想法，先举手 / 老师请到我，再站起来 / 声音响亮，说完整的话
- Visual: one pupil raising a hand correctly.
- Interaction: judge which hand-raising posture is appropriate.

### 14. Speaking and listening

- Title: 勇敢表达，也要学会倾听
- Text: 轮到我时，大方说 / 别人发言时，认真听 / 不同的想法，也值得尊重
- Visual: small classroom sharing circle.

### 15. Learning tools

- Title: 爱护我的学习用品
- Text: 课本轻轻翻 / 铅笔不乱扔 / 用完放回原处 / 桌面保持整洁
- Visual: tidy and untidy desks, with emphasis on the tidy example.

### 16. Schoolbag organization

- Title: 我是书包整理小能手
- Text: 大书放下面 / 小书放上面 / 文具放进文具盒 / 水杯放在固定位置
- Visual: open schoolbag with sorted items.
- Interaction: say where each item belongs.

### 17. Recess priorities

- Title: 课间十分钟，先做三件事
- Text: 先上卫生间 / 再喝水 / 准备下一节课
- Closing: 做完这些，再轻松休息。
- Visual: three connected scenes.

### 18. Queueing

- Title: 排队做到快、静、齐
- Text: 听到口令，快速集合 / 不推挤，不插队 / 前后保持合适距离
- Visual: orderly line outside the classroom.
- Interaction: practise one quiet queue.

### 19. Corridor and stairs

- Title: 走廊和楼梯慢慢走
- Text: 走廊不奔跑 / 上下楼梯靠右走 / 不推人，不拉人 / 转弯处放慢脚步
- Visual: correct corridor and stair behaviour with green checks.

### 20. Playground safety

- Title: 操场活动，安全第一
- Text: 在规定区域活动 / 运动前先热身 / 不做危险动作 / 身体不舒服马上停下
- Visual: orderly skipping, running, and ball games.
- Interaction: judge safe and unsafe actions.

### 21. Health and hygiene

- Title: 讲卫生，身体棒
- Text: 饭前便后要洗手 / 咳嗽喷嚏遮住口鼻 / 水杯自己用 / 不舒服及时告诉老师
- Visual: four small hygiene scenes.

### 22. Dismissal safety

- Title: 放学安全要记牢
- Text: 听从老师安排 / 排队走到指定位置 / 只跟约定好的家人离开 / 不跟陌生人走
- Visual: teacher handing pupils to guardians at the school gate.
- Interaction: answer "可以" or "不可以" for short scenarios.

### 23. Friendship

- Title: 友好相处，班级更温暖
- Text: 主动说你好 / 愿意一起玩 / 学会分享 / 看到别人需要时，伸出小手
- Visual: sharing books, playing together, and helping pick up stationery.
- Interaction: compliment a nearby classmate.

### 24. Small conflicts

- Title: 发生小矛盾，我可以这样做
- Text: 先停下来 / 把事情说清楚 / 听听对方怎么想 / 一起找解决办法
- Visual: two pupils talking calmly with teacher guidance.

### 25. Polite words

- Title: 三句有礼貌的魔法话
- Text: 请 / 谢谢 / 对不起
- Closing: 有礼貌的话，会让大家更愿意帮助我。
- Visual: borrowing stationery, receiving help, and apologizing.
- Interaction: rehearse one scene.

### 26. Ask for help

- Title: 遇到困难，我会主动求助
- Text: 我不会，可以请教 / 我受伤了，要马上告诉老师 / 我找不到家人，可以找老师或保安 / 我心里难过，也可以说出来
- Visual: asking a teacher, school nurse, and security guard for help.

### 27. Calm down

- Title: 让心情慢慢平静下来
- Text: 停一停 / 慢慢呼吸 / 说出我的感受 / 找值得信任的人帮忙
- Visual: child breathing slowly with teacher support.
- Interaction: take three slow breaths together.

### 28. Reading

- Title: 每天读一点，知识多一点
- Text: 固定一个阅读时间 / 选择喜欢的图书 / 安静、专心地阅读 / 读完说说自己的发现
- Visual: warm classroom reading corner.

### 29. Simple tasks

- Title: 小任务，我会认真完成
- Text: 听清任务 / 准备好用品 / 自己先试一试 / 完成以后认真检查
- Closing: 不会的地方，可以请教老师。
- Visual: pupil completing a simple task at a quiet desk; adult supports without replacing the child.

### 30. Promise and ending

- Title: 新学期，我准备好了！
- Text: 我会认真听讲 / 我会遵守规则 / 我会友好待人 / 我会保护自己
- Closing: 你好，一年级！我们一起快乐成长！
- Visual: class group in front of a growth tree with cards reading 认真、勇敢、友爱、安全.
- Interaction: read the promise together.

## 5. NotebookLM prompt construction

When producing a copy-ready NotebookLM prompt, assemble it in this order:

1. State the exact artifact: "生成一套30页、16:9比例的小学一年级新生开学第一课PPT".
2. State the title, use case, audience, and learning goals.
3. Define the visual system from section 3.
4. Add these global constraints:
   - keep every slide;
   - do not merge or add slides;
   - use matching illustrations on every content slide;
   - keep body text to four short points or fewer;
   - separate text and illustration;
   - keep recurring characters consistent;
   - do not use training-camp or heavy level-clearing language;
   - do not print structural labels such as "标题", "正文", "画面", or "互动" on slides;
   - add 1-2 teacher-note sentences when speaker notes are supported.
5. Insert the 30-page specification from section 4.
6. End by requiring exactly 30 complete slides and no extra contents page.

When the user requests fewer pages, preserve this order of importance:

1. Emotional safety and identity.
2. Teachers, classmates, campus, and school-day rhythm.
3. Sitting, listening, raising a hand, speaking, and schoolbag organization.
4. Recess, queueing, campus safety, and dismissal safety.
5. Friendship, conflict handling, help-seeking, reading/tasks, and promise.

Do not shorten by removing all interactions or by collapsing safety into a dense text slide.

## 6. Commercial package

When the request is product-oriented, recommend:

- Editable 16:9 PPT.
- Complete lesson plan.
- Teacher talk track or verbatim script.
- Printable class promise card.
- Schoolbag organization card.
- Feeling-weather card.
- Safety judgement cards.
- Optional 30-slide standard edition and 40-60-slide extended activity edition.

Use a cover promise such as:

- 一年级新生开学第一课
- 30页直接用
- 含教案与逐字稿
- 新手班主任不冷场

Do not use false urgency, unverifiable download claims, or engagement numbers as teaching-quality proof.

## 7. Final checks

- Does the first third reduce uncertainty before presenting rules?
- Can pupils repeat or act out the visible wording?
- Does each rule appear in a concrete school scene?
- Is there one light interaction every 2-3 slides?
- Are safety and help-seeking both present?
- Are characters, colors, and illustration style consistent?
- Does the final promise feel encouraging rather than punitive?
- Can a teacher use the deck without rewriting dense administrative language?
- If it is a commercial product, are editable files, lesson plan, talk track, and printable cards clearly specified?
